Third-party product interfaces, names, logos and trademarks

Some demo films in styles/*/demo/ name or redraw third-party products, companies
or services (for example AI model and company names in the Rhythm Game Show and
Broadcast News Package demos). These names, interfaces, logos and trademarks
belong to their owners. They are excluded from every licence in this repository,
and no licence is given here for them. Anyone reusing them needs their owners'
permission or another legal basis, even when they stay inside a complete picture
or film.
